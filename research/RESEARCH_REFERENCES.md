## Principles adopted

1. **Palette discipline.** Warm white dominates (walls, ceilings), warm greys and greige come second, beige and taupe go in textiles and joinery, and walnut, charcoal and brass are limited accents. One timber family (oak + walnut) is repeated across every room.
2. **Low, modular seating with a single rug.** The living room has one sectional and one accent chair. Shadow-gap plinths keep the furniture visually light, and nothing crowds the path to the balcony.
3. **Feature walls carry the drama.** The fluted timber headboard wall, the walnut-slat and stone media wall and the fluted kitchen portal are features; other walls stay quiet.
4. **Layered light.** Ambient (2700–3000 K coves and recessed linear profiles) + task (downlights, under-cabinet strips, desk lamps) + accent (gimbal spots on art, slats and the shrine). The bedrooms use 2700 K; the office uses 4000 K.
5. **Deoghar.** Ivory stone, a warm-wood arch, side jaali panels, brass bells and diyas, and a concealed 2700 K strip. It is never colour-lit.
6. **Bathrooms.** Large-format light stone tile, a wet zone behind clear glass with a niche, a wall-hung WC with a concealed cistern, a backlit mirror, and a wall-hung vanity.
7. **Indian household practicality.** A shoe console at the entry, deep drawers, a tall pantry and chimney, a utility area with a washer, sink, broom store and pulley dryer, and ceiling fans.
8. **Sliding wardrobes everywhere.** Full height, concealed edge handles, taupe/oak panels with limited smoked mirror.

## Volume II upgrade (styles, scenes, mandir)

9. **2026 colour direction.** Warm, earthy and deep tones replace cool greys: deep espresso browns (Benjamin Moore Silhouette), muddied mosses and card-room greens, plum with smoky mauve (Carter Plum / Peignoir), inky navy, warm greige (Revere Pewter) and soft architectural whites. These became 10 new palettes (16 in total).
10. **Tile and floor trends.** Hand-made zellige, herringbone and chevron parquet, warm terrazzo, patterned encaustic cement, book-matched marble and regional stones (Kota, Jaisalmer). These became 11 new floors (19 in total), generated procedurally.
11. **One colour temperature per room.** 2200–2700 K for intimate and evening use, 2700–3000 K for living, 3000–3500 K for kitchens and gallery lighting, and 4000 K for task lighting. Saturated colour appears only in coves and accent strips, through the colour wheel or scenes such as Blue hour, Cinema, Spa and Diwali.
12. **Mandir.** It sits in the north-east (Vastu) and is layered as follows:
    - A backlit onyx backdrop, a cusped marble arch with brass inlay and a kalash finial.
    - A Chaturbhuja Vishnu murti in samapada stance on a double-lotus pitha. It carries the shankha, chakra, gada and padma, and wears a kirita mukuta, a vanamala and a pitambara.
    - Diyas, samai lamps, temple bells, a marigold toran, a rangoli and an urli.
